## Shared guardrails (already embedded in every prompt below)
- **Brand palette:** heritage pine green `#1B3A2F`, harvest gold `#D9A441`, terracotta clay `#C2562F`, sage green `#8FA68E`, warm bone/cream `#F7F3EA`.
- **Use case:** small chat-bubble mascot, must read clearly at ~60px — bold simple silhouette, clean shapes, strong figure-to-ground contrast.
- **Output:** single centered character, **transparent background**, **no text/letters/numbers** in the image, friendly three-quarter front view, soft and approachable, full character or head-and-shoulders as noted, 1:1 square.
- **Tone:** optimistic, welcoming, capable — never spooky, doom, military, or "prepper."
- Tip: for a set, regenerate each winner with expression variants (waving hello, thumbs-up, thinking, celebrating) for the chat UI states.
